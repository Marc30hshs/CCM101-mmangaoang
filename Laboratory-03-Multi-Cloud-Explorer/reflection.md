
# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

## My Reflection

Completing Mission 3 gave me a better understanding of how cloud computing platforms are used in real-world situations. Among the three providers, **AWS impressed me the most** because of its wide selection of services and the flexibility it offers for different types of projects. I found it interesting that a single platform can provide tools for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and many other requirements.

Although AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have different names and interfaces, they have many **similarities**. All three provide virtual machines, cloud storage, databases, networking, identity management, and other services needed to build cloud-based applications. They also allow organizations to increase or decrease resources depending on their workload.

I learned that **business requirements have a major effect on cloud platform selection**. A company should not simply choose a provider because it is popular. Factors such as budget, existing technology, application requirements, security, scalability, location, and future business plans should be considered. For example, Azure can be a good choice for organizations already using Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud can be suitable for AI, data, and Kubernetes workloads.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** helped me understand that recommending a cloud platform requires analyzing the client's situation first. I learned to connect technical services with actual business needs instead of simply listing cloud features.

Finally, this mission improved my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** by adding organized research, comparisons, client recommendations, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and documentation. My repository now shows not only what I learned about cloud platforms but also how I can apply that knowledge to different scenarios. Overall, this mission increased my confidence in understanding and evaluating cloud technologies.
